## How BeWild’s Claude Subscription Process Works

BeWild’s current Claude tutorial describes a four-stage process:

1. Choose the Claude plan and subscription duration.
2. Log into your own Claude account.
3. Export the required Claude cookie data.
4. Confirm the order and complete payment.

The platform says the process usually takes approximately three to five minutes, with activation commonly taking five to thirty minutes after payment. Those are BeWild’s stated process estimates, not a guarantee for every account or network.

The account connection step is the part that deserves the most attention. For Claude subscriptions, BeWild instructs users to log into `claude.ai` and export cookies in JSON format using a browser extension. The exported data is then pasted into the BeWild subscription page.

A browser cookie can contain active login information. That means users should understand exactly what they are submitting, use only the official BeWild page, avoid sharing the exported data with anyone else, and avoid using a browser extension they do not trust.

The practical flow is:

### Choose the plan

Open the BeWild subscription page through the supplied campaign link, select Claude, and choose Pro, Max 5x, or Max 20x.

For a single month, choose the one-month option. For a longer order, check whether the selected option creates a multi-month balance or a recurring renewal arrangement.

### Confirm the network environment

BeWild’s tutorial states that Claude can be sensitive to network conditions and recommends checking the network before proceeding. It also advises users to keep the network location consistent during activation and later use.

This is not a reason to assume that a VPN or a particular country will always solve access problems. Network behavior can change, and users should not claim a location they are not entitled to use or violate Claude’s terms.

### Export the required cookie data

The tutorial says the cookie should be exported from the Claude website while logged into the intended account. Copying data from a search engine page or another website will not work.

Double-check the account email shown during the process. A subscription applied to the wrong account can be difficult or impossible to transfer.

### Review the final order

## What to Check Before Buying a Claude Pro Discount

A genuine discount should survive a basic comparison. Use this checklist before completing payment.

### Compare the same billing period

Compare one month with one month, or one year with one year. A third-party service may show a low monthly equivalent that requires a multi-month upfront payment.

For example, an advertised “monthly” price could require payment for three months at once. That is not necessarily bad, but it is different from a flexible monthly subscription.

Some offers reduce only the first payment. Others provide a lower price for the entire selected duration. Ask:

- Will the next month renew at a different price?
- Is the plan automatically renewed?

That makes the pre-payment review especially important. Check the plan, duration, account, and price before confirming.

Before using any third-party subscription service, consider whether you are comfortable:
